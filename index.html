ABOUT

Fredsgiving Rave is a free student event presented by Courtland Entertainment. The night brings together music, student organizations, community, and live event experience in one space.

Expect electronic music, lighting effects, participating student organizations, and a full rave-style atmosphere.

Admission is free, but RSVP is required because attendance is limited.
