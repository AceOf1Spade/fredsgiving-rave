ABOUT THE EVENT

Fredsgiving Rave is a high-energy electronic dance music fundraiser designed to bring students and community members together for a fun, social night while raising funds for Courtland Entertainment.

The event will feature live DJs, electronic dance music, dancing, and a rave-inspired atmosphere focused on music, community, and supporting the club.
